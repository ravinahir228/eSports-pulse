GAME OVERVIEW

What is BGMI?

Battlegrounds Mobile India (BGMI) is a free-to-play multiplayer battle royale game developed and published by KRAFTON. Players land on a battleground, collect weapons and equipment, and fight to survive until the end of the match.

Players can team up with friends, choose first-person or third-person perspectives, explore different maps, use vehicles and develop their own combat strategies. Map availability and gameplay features may change with game updates.

100 Players in a classic battle royale match
FPP / TPP First-person and third-person perspectives
Android / iOS Mobile platforms

Gameplay & Features

Learn the fundamentals of BGMI.

🎯

Battle Royale

Land on the map, collect supplies, survive the shrinking safe zone and try to be the last player or squad standing.

🗺️

Maps & Locations

Explore different battlegrounds, learn important locations and plan your rotations. Available maps can vary by mode and update.

🔫

Weapons & Equipment

Find firearms, ammunition, attachments, armour and healing supplies. Choose equipment that fits your playstyle and situation.

🚙

Vehicles

Use available vehicles to travel, rotate between locations and reposition your squad during matches.

👥

Team Strategy

Communicate with teammates, share resources, coordinate pushes and choose smart positions to improve your chances of survival.
